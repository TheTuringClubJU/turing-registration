# Contributors

The Turing Club website and registration platform is built by its members. Thank you to everyone who has contributed code, design, content, and ideas. ❤️

This list is maintained by hand alongside the GitHub [contributors graph](https://github.com/TheTuringClubJU/turing-registration/graphs/contributors). Merged a PR and don't see yourself here? Open a PR adding your line — that counts too, and each entry here is eligible for a certificate of contribution.
